Our Partnership & Mission

About Team Community Spark & SDSS

Team Community Spark is an academic community initiative from K.R. Mangalam University working directly with Shanti Devi Shiksha Samiti (SDSS) in Gurugram. Our goal is to replace slow, paper-based shelter tracking with responsive, zero-cost digital tools that protect vulnerable children from silent malnutrition.

Evidence-Based Rationale

Why WHO Standards Are Our Evaluation Priority

We don't use WHO benchmarks just to show a calculator—we use them because early childhood stunting (height-for-age) and wasting (weight-for-height) cause irreversible cognitive and physical deficits if left unaddressed before age 5.

1. Targeted High-Risk Priority

Identifies children in 'Red Flag' zones so caregivers can immediately double protein and calorie rations.

2. Coordinated Kitchen Interventions

Links shelter growth records directly with kitchen cooks to tailor daily dal, milk, and micro-nutrients.

3. Longitudinal Recovery Tracking

Tracks monthly progress to ensure children move safely from Yellow/Red alert status to healthy green curve zones.

WHO Pediatric Growth Evaluator

Input child metrics to evaluate standard growth status against global WHO curves.
