Our Core Purpose

OUR MISSION

Shanti Devi Shiksha Samiti (SDSS) operates with a singular commitment: to eradicate childhood malnutrition and create sustainable pathways for holistic growth. Inspired by the selfless service model of Narayan Seva Sansthan, we combine on-ground shelter relief with digital precision.

Multi-Layered Care Model

OUR APPROACH

Our integrated framework ensures that every child receives immediate nutritional support along with long-term developmental tracking.

Pillar 01

Daily Caloric & Nutrient Balance

We curate freshly prepared meal boxes daily at our community kitchens. Designed by clinical nutritionists, each meal guarantees mandatory RDA quotas for proteins, iron, calcium, and vitamin A for developing bodies.

  • Fortified pulses, green leafy vegetables, and whole grains.
  • Specialized supplements for severely acute malnourished (SAM) children.
  • Zero-waste hygienic central kitchen standards.
Pillar 02

Digital Growth Tracking App

In collaboration with Team Community Spark (K.R. Mangalam University), caregivers utilize a custom offline-first digital app to benchmark every child against WHO growth standard curves.

  • Instant Height-for-Age and Weight-for-Height percentile calculations.
  • Automatic alert triggers for children requiring specialized medical diet.
  • QR-based digital identity for seamless progress logging.
Pillar 03

Regular Medical Camps & Hygiene Drives

Nutrition without health hygiene is incomplete. SDSS organizes bi-weekly pediatric doctor visits, deworming drives, and clean water distribution camps across shelter homes.

  • Pediatric health checkups and free distribution of vitamins.
  • Hygiene kits (soaps, toothbrushes, sanitizers) provided monthly.
  • Clean drinking water filtration setups in slum centers.
Pillar 04

Education & Foundational Literacy

Once physical health stabilizes, we transition children into evening learning circles, preparing them for formal schooling enrollment and basic digital literacy.

  • Free books, stationery, and learning aids.
  • University student volunteers providing weekend tutoring.
  • Mainstream school admissions assistance.
