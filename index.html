ABOUT SHANTI DEVI SHIKSHA SAMITI & COMMUNITY SPARK

Creating sustainable, nutrition-focused community intervention frameworks for early childhood development.

Our Shared Vision

Shanti Devi Shiksha Samiti (SDSS) works tirelessly in rural sectors to provide underprivileged children with holistic education, primary nutrition, and healthcare support.

Through partnership with Team Community Spark at K.R. Mangalam University, we combine youth dynamism, data analytics, and modern web software to scale this grassroots impact.
